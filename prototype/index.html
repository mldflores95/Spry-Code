On March 14, 2023, the Respondent Agency, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS), Pacific Southwest Regional Office, conducted a structured competitive interview process for Vacancy Announcement DOI-FWS-2023-0312, a GS-12 Senior Wildlife Biologist position located in Sacramento, California. ROI p.44, ¶2 ROI p.44, ¶2 — "...supervisory GS-15 Division Chief convened a structured interview panel comprising three members for the purpose of filling Vacancy Announcement DOI-FWS-2023-0312..." The panel consisted of three agency officials, and fourteen (14) applicants were certified from the competitive certificate of eligibles.

Complainant, a GS-11 Wildlife Biologist with eleven (11) years of federal service, was among the certified applicants. Complainant holds a Master of Science degree in wildlife ecology and possessed direct, specialized experience in Pacific salmon habitat assessment substantially consistent with the stated requirements of the vacancy. ROI p.47, ¶3 ROI p.47, ¶3 — "...Complainant's application materials reflected a Master of Science degree in wildlife ecology and demonstrated direct experience in Pacific salmon habitat assessment..." Complainant received a numerical interview score of 84 out of 100.

The Selecting Official selected a GS-11 employee with six (6) years of federal service and no advanced degree, who received a numerical interview score of 78 out of 100 — six points below Complainant's score. The Selecting Official's contemporaneous selection documentation does not reference the score differential or the selectee's specialized experience as the basis for the selection decision. ROI p.112, ¶1 ROI p.112, ¶1 — "...selectee received a numerical interview score of 78 out of 100, compared to Complainant's score of 84 out of 100. The Selecting Official's contemporaneous notes do not reference the selectee's specialized experience..."

During the pre-complaint counseling stage, Complainant alleged that the Selecting Official made a post-panel remark indicating that the division needed someone who "fits in culturally with the existing team," which Complainant identified as a potentially discriminatory statement based on race. The Selecting Official testified during the investigation that this remark referred to collaborative work style and denied that race was a factor in the selection. ROI p.118, ¶2 ROI p.118, ¶2 — "...Complainant identified the Selecting Official's post-panel statement...The Selecting Official later testified that this statement referred to 'collaborative work style, not race or national origin'..." Complainant filed a formal EEO complaint on June 3, 2023, alleging discrimination on the basis of race (African American) in the Respondent's failure to select Complainant for the GS-12 position.
